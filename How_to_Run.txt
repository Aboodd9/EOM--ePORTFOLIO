VALIDATION LIMITS
The primary test preserves time order within each user, not globally.
The three development folds use inner validation for early stopping and a
separate outer evaluation fold. They assess partition sensitivity, not global
time generalisation. Activity groups use training history only.
NDCG@10 compares one held-out positive against up to 100 unlabelled films.
Unknown or untrained identities require a fallback and are not a solved cold
start problem. Historical demographic fields support limited diagnostics only.
